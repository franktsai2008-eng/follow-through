Buyer required 200 custom-printed double-wall corrugated cartons delivered within 30 days and was willing to exceed $44 per unit to meet quantity and timing. We offered 150 units at $44 each, free freight, net-30 terms, shipping on day 40, with split shipment available. Buyer rejected these terms because both 200 units and the 30-day window were firm. No order was placed. The additional 50 units, alternate sourcing, or any shipment before day 40 would require written sales director approval, which was unavailable during the conversation. Opportunity is closed for now, with possible future business if the buyer has a longer lead time.
